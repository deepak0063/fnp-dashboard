# FNP Sales Analysis – Executive Summary

## 1. Project Overview

The **FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive view of sales performance using a dataset of **1,000 orders**. The analysis focuses on revenue, customer spending, delivery time, product categories, occasions, cities, days of the week, and order time.

The objective of this project is to identify major revenue contributors, customer purchasing patterns, high-performing categories and occasions, and areas that can support better business and operational decisions.

---

## 2. Overall Business Performance

The dashboard reports the following key performance indicators:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | **1,000** |
| Total Revenue | **₹35,20,984** |
| Average Customer Spending | **₹3,520.98** |
| Average Delivery Time | **5.53 days** |

The average customer spending of **₹3,520.98 per order** indicates that customers are making relatively high-value purchases. The average delivery time of **5.53 days** provides an important operational metric for evaluating delivery efficiency.

## 7. Revenue by Order Time

The order-time trend shows considerable variation throughout the day. The dashboard indicates several higher-revenue periods during the afternoon and evening, with noticeable activity around **6 PM–8 PM**.

### Key Insight

The time-based analysis can help identify high-demand ordering windows. Marketing campaigns and promotional notifications can be scheduled around historically stronger periods.

However, revenue should ideally be analyzed together with **order count and average order value**, because a revenue peak may result from either more orders or higher-value orders.

## 8. Overall Business Insights

### Revenue Concentration

A relatively small number of categories and occasions contribute a substantial portion of overall revenue. **Colors, Soft Toys, Sweets, Anniversary, and Raksha Bandhan** are particularly important contributors in the displayed data.

### High Customer Spending

The average customer spending of approximately **₹3,520.98** creates an opportunity to increase revenue further through bundles, complementary products, and personalized recommendations.

### Occasion-Driven Purchasing

Revenue varies across occasions, indicating that customer demand is influenced by gifting occasions. Seasonal and occasion-specific campaigns can therefore play an important role.

### Geographic Variation

Order activity differs across cities, creating opportunities for location-specific marketing and operational planning.

### Time-Based Purchasing Behavior

Revenue fluctuates throughout the day, with some afternoon and evening periods showing relatively higher activity. Understanding these patterns can help optimize campaign timing.

### Delivery Performance

The average delivery time of **5.53 days** is an important operational KPI. Tracking delivery time alongside customer satisfaction, cancellation rates, and repeat purchases would provide a better understanding of its business impact.

---

## 9. Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on High-Performing Categories

Maintain strong inventory and product availability for categories such as **Colors, Soft Toys, and Sweets**.

### 2. Strengthen Occasion-Based Campaigns

Use targeted campaigns around high-revenue occasions such as **Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan**, while investigating why other occasions perform differently.

### 3. Increase Average Order Value

Introduce product bundles and cross-selling opportunities, such as combining cakes, sweets, gifts, and personalized products.

### 4. Use City-Level Targeting

Develop localized campaigns for cities with higher order activity while testing customer acquisition campaigns in lower-performing locations.

### 5. Optimize Campaign Timing

Use order-time analysis to schedule marketing messages and promotions around periods with stronger historical demand.

### 6. Monitor Delivery Efficiency

Track the **5.53-day average delivery time** by city, product category, and order volume to identify areas where logistics can be improved.

---

## 10. Executive Summary

The FNP Sales Analysis project analyzes **1,000 customer orders** to understand sales performance, customer spending patterns, product-category performance, occasion-based demand, geographic distribution, order timing, and delivery efficiency. The dataset generated approximately **₹35.21 lakh in total revenue**, with an average customer spending of **₹3,520.98 per order** and an average delivery time of **5.53 days**.

The analysis indicates that revenue is concentrated across specific product categories and gifting occasions. **Colors** is the highest-revenue category, followed by **Soft Toys and Sweets**, while **Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan** are among the strongest-performing occasions. Revenue also varies across days of the week, with **Tuesday and Sunday** showing relatively higher revenue in the dashboard.

Geographic analysis shows differences in order activity across cities, providing opportunities for region-specific marketing and operational planning. The time-based analysis also reveals fluctuations in customer purchasing activity throughout the day, particularly during afternoon and evening periods.

Overall, the dashboard provides a data-driven view of FNP's sales performance and can support decisions related to **inventory planning, occasion-based marketing, customer targeting, regional campaigns, promotional timing, and delivery optimization**. Further analysis of order volume, average order value, customer segments, and delivery performance at a more granular level could provide additional insights for business growth.
